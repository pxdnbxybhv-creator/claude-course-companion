# Responsible Use

Claude Course Companion is designed to support learning, not replace it.

The prompts in this repository are structured so that Claude builds understanding step by step, asks you to answer before giving feedback, and focuses on your reasoning rather than on producing a finished answer. That structure only helps if you use it in the same spirit.

## Good practices

Students should:

- Verify important technical claims against course materials or trusted references.
- Attempt problems independently before asking AI for complete solutions.
- Use explanations to understand reasoning, not just copy answers.
- Treat AI output as guidance rather than an authoritative source.
- Follow course and university academic integrity policies.
- Avoid submitting AI-generated work as their own.
- Protect private, confidential, or sensitive information.
- Avoid uploading copyrighted material publicly without permission.

## Things to keep in mind

- Claude can be confidently wrong, including when grading your quiz answers. A confident tone is not evidence of correctness.
- Your course may define terms, use notation, or emphasize methods differently from general explanations. When they disagree, your course is the reference for your exams.
- Different courses and institutions have different rules about AI use. If you are not sure whether a particular use is allowed, ask your instructor.

## A useful rule

Before asking AI:

"What is the answer?"

consider asking:

"What do I need to understand to solve this?"

The goal of this project is to make AI a better learning partner, not a shortcut around learning.
