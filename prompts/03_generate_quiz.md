# 03 — Generate a Diagnostic Quiz

## Usage

Use this after completing a learning section.

Send this prompt in the same conversation so that the quiz is based on what you actually studied, not on general knowledge of the subject.

## Prompt

```text
Based only on the material we have studied in this conversation, generate a short diagnostic quiz.

Include:

- 3 conceptual understanding questions
- 2 application or reasoning questions
- 1 question designed around a common misconception

Do not provide the answers yet.

Ask me to answer all questions first.

After I answer:

For each question:

1. Mark my answer as:
   - correct
   - partially correct
   - incorrect
2. Explain what I understood correctly.
3. Explain what I misunderstood or missed.
4. Identify the concept connected to the mistake.
5. Give a concise corrected explanation.

Then provide:

- my strongest area
- my weakest area
- the 2–3 concepts I should review next

Do not evaluate writing style unless it affects technical correctness.

Focus on understanding.
```

## Tips

- Answer in your own words without looking at your notes. The quiz is a diagnostic tool, so it is most useful when it reveals real gaps.
- If you are unsure, say so in your answer rather than guessing. This helps the feedback distinguish between a knowledge gap and a lucky guess.
- Quiz grading by an AI can be wrong. If a "correction" contradicts your course material, check the course material first.
